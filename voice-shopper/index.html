Voice Shoppersimulation

Talk to the marketplace the way you would talk to a voice assistant. State a goal, a budget, a product type and a minimum rating — the shopper plans the request, calls AgentMart's live search_listings, get_listing and get_reviews tools, and shows you every call it made. Nothing here is canned: if the catalogue has no match, the shopper says so.
